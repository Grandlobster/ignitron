PROMPT CHALLENGE

AI-Generated Image Competition

Welcome to IGNITRON 2K25 Prompt Challenge. Participants must generate images based on assigned prompts. Your creations will be evaluated by AI Agents using a tree-of-prompts evaluation and chain-of-thought reasoning to determine creativity, prompt adherence, and overall quality.

How It Works

🖌️
Generate Image
Create an AI-generated image strictly following the assigned textual prompt.
🤖
AI Evaluation
Images are analyzed via a tree-of-prompts model using AI agents to score relevance and creativity.
🧠
Chain-of-Thought
AI chains evaluate reasoning behind prompt interpretation and image generation.

Rules

  • Each participant submits 1 image per prompt.
  • Images must be generated via AI, no manual editing allowed.
  • Prompts are assigned at registration and must be strictly followed.
  • AI Agents will evaluate adherence, creativity, and prompt complexity.
  • Decision of AI evaluation is final and cannot be contested.
  • All submissions must be original and unique for the competition.

Submission Guidelines

  • Submit images in JPG/PNG format, maximum 5MB.
  • Include your prompt and brief explanation of your approach.
  • Use the official submission form link below.
  • Ensure your AI generation metadata is preserved (if possible).
