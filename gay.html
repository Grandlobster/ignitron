DAY 1 - CHALLENGES

01

AI PROMPT GAME

Explore the power of artificial intelligence through creative prompt challenges. Participants will craft innovative instructions and test AI outputs for wit, accuracy, and originality. A battle of imagination where the smartest prompt takes the prize.

02

ESCAPE ROOM

Step into a world of mystery and puzzles designed to challenge your problem-solving skills. Teams must crack codes, solve riddles, and uncover hidden clues before time runs out. Collaboration and sharp thinking are the keys to escape.

03

RAPID CODING

Put your coding speed and accuracy to the ultimate test. Participants will be given short coding problems that must be solved within strict time limits. Precision, logic, and quick execution decide the winners in this adrenaline-filled contest.

DAY 2 - MASTERY

01

HACKATHON

Teams collaborate over hours of brainstorming, coding, and designing to build innovative projects. Participants will tackle real-world problems, prototype solutions, and pitch their ideas to judges. Creativity, teamwork, and technical excellence fuel this flagship event.

02

UI/UX DESIGN

Showcase creativity and design thinking by building clean, user-friendly digital experiences. Competitors will create wireframes or mockups that emphasize clarity, aesthetics, and usability. A challenge where innovation meets design precision.

03

DATA WRANGLER

Transform raw, unorganized data into meaningful insights. Participants will clean, structure, and analyze messy datasets using logic and tools. Success depends on accuracy, attention to detail, and the ability to turn chaos into clarity.

04

BLIND CODING

A test of pure coding logic where screens go dark. Participants must write error-free code without the aid of compilers or real-time debugging. Only true mastery of syntax, logic, and memory ensures victory in this challenge.

05

DEBUG HUNT

Hunt down bugs and fix broken code under pressure. Competitors are tasked with identifying errors and optimizing programs within time limits. The one who restores functionality fastest while keeping code clean and efficient earns victory.

06

ESPORTS & CHESS

Strategy meets competition in digital arenas and classic board battles. Participants showcase tactical thinking, quick reflexes, and strategic mastery. From virtual battlefields to checkered boards, prove your supremacy in mind and skill.
